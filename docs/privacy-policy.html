Retro Race – Privacy Policy

Developer: CSLab (Virgil Ciociu) · Effective date: 26 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android game Retro Race (package com.cslab.retrorace) handles your information.

Information we collect

Retro Race does not collect, store or share any personal information. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

Permissions

The game does not request any special Android permissions. It does not use the internet, location, camera, microphone or storage outside of its own app folder.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, best lap times and settings are saved only on your own device. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising and third-party services

The current version of Retro Race does not contain advertising, analytics or other third-party services. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will list the services used and the data they collect.

Children

Retro Race does not knowingly collect any information from children or from anyone else.

Open source

Retro Race is based on the open-source game Pixel Wheels by Aurélien Gâteau. The full source code of Retro Race is available at github.com/virgilciociu/retro-race.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
