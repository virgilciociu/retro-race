## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Race to the finish line in Retro Race, a fast and fun top-down racing game with classic pixel-art style!

Pick your car, choose a track and race against tough opponents. Grab bonuses on the road, fire missiles, drop mines and use turbo boosts to get ahead. Easy to pick up, hard to master!

FEATURES
• Quick Race – jump straight into the action
• Championships – win a series of races to become the champion
• Many tracks: city streets, snowy roads, countryside, rivers and the seaside
• Lots of cars to unlock
• Bonuses and weapons: missiles, mines, gun and turbo boosts
• Several difficulty levels, from Casual to Hard
• Classic retro pixel-art graphics and music
• Works offline – no internet needed
• Small download, runs smoothly on older phones

Retro Race is made by CSLab and is based on the open-source game Pixel Wheels by Aurélien Gâteau. Source code: github.com/virgilciociu/retro-race
